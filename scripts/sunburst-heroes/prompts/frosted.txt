Conservatively retouch the supplied original product photograph.
This is a restoration of an existing photograph, not a new rendering.

The original image is the authority for the product’s shape, material,
colour, exposure, and surface shading.

PRIMARY REQUIREMENT — PRESERVE THE FROSTED BODY
Preserve the bottle body’s existing continuous milky, pale gray-white
finish exactly as it appears in the original.

The white finish is part of the product. It is not haze, blur, dirt,
overexposure, or background that needs to be removed.

Retain the original pale gray-white midtones across the broad central
face, along with the original soft shading at the shoulders, sides,
and base. Keep the centre visibly present as a finished surface.

Do not brighten the body, lift its midtones toward the background
colour, increase transparency, or replace its soft surface shading
with clear-glass reflections. Do not create a glossy transparent rim
or a clearer base than the original.

Preserve the existing surface texture. Do not invent additional
grain, mottling, sparkling particles, or sandblasted texture.

PERMITTED CHANGES
Reduce obvious compression artifacts and conservatively clarify
existing details. Keep the body’s overall exposure and tonal
gradients unchanged. When detail is ambiguous, preserve the original
rather than inventing it.

Preserve all component colours and finishes. Clarify existing details
in the cap, atomizer, cord, or tassel only where those components
are present. Do not restyle them.

PRESERVE COMPOSITION
Keep the original silhouette, proportions, framing, relative scale,
placement, and component arrangement. Add or remove nothing.

FOR THIS TEST
Leave the background, lighting direction, and shadows unchanged.
Do not apply global relighting, whitening, dehazing, or a new
editorial photography style.

Success means the original product looks slightly cleaner and
better defined while retaining the same visible material.
A sharper image with a different finish is a failed result.
