# Style-Reference Shoot — Studio Brief (printable, pin in studio)

## 10 shots, one canonical setup

**Lighting (constant across every shot):**
- Key: large softbox, upper camera-left, ~30° elevation, ~5200K
- Fill: white card or small softbox, camera-right, ~4800K, ~1.5 stops below key
- Edge card: white card behind subject, slightly camera-left, ~30cm back
- No kicker, no second rim, no atmospheric haze

**Camera:** dead-on front, eye-level with mid-shoulder, ~85mm macro, **f/11**, ISO 100, tripod, 2s timer

**Background:** Bone `#F5F3EF` paper, edge to edge, 1-2% vertical gradient, soft ~20% contact shadow, no horizon line, no tabletop edge, no vignette, no paper texture.

**Settings:** RAW, convert to sRGB PNG. Output **2080×2288** minimum (4096×4576 preferred).

## Forbidden

- ❌ No labels, logos, text anywhere
- ❌ No hands, models, props
- ❌ No colored gel on key (color from glass, not light)
- ❌ No heavy post-processing (no LUT, no saturation boost, no vignette)
- ❌ No two caps where the brief says one
- ❌ No sprayer on the Apothecary (the closure IS the stopper)
