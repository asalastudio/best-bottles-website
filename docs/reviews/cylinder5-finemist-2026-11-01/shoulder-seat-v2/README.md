# Shoulder seating revision

The user asked for the narrower cap/collar proportion in the supplied photographs, with a very slight band of neck visible above the shoulder.

- Raised the common sprayer and capped position by 21 registered canvas units from the earlier shoulder-span landmark. The bottle scale and landmark itself are unchanged.
- Overcaps are scaled uniformly to roughly 102% of the sprayer collar width. A minimum height of actuator height plus 0.3 mm of visual clearance prevents the source actuator protruding above a capped assembly. The widest overcap is less than 105% of its sprayer width.
- Recomputed the shared tube length to keep the mechanism attached and the tip fixed above the inner floor. Width stays at the 1.1 mm visual assumption.
- Both clear and cobalt receive the same component placement. Prior kits and the initial raised-seat candidate are retained here.

The final `*-neck.png` and `*-neck-detail.png` images show the narrow cap/collar and thin exposed neck. Files without `-neck` are the intermediate seating candidate, not the final version.

Verification is recorded in `evidence/`; targeted geometry and shared-source tests are in the adjacent cobalt material batch. Local review only.
