This local review changes 55 exact configurations: fifteen 9 ml / 17-415 lotion pumps, thirty-six Flair 15 ml / 13-415 assemblies, and four tall Cylinder 9 ml / 13-415 short ribbed caps. Bottle imagery, approved sizing, external lotion pump heads/collars/overcaps and roller seating are preserved.

The lotion mechanism was generated with the built-in image editor using the supplied lotion photograph and earlier mechanism reference. The owner accepted the slimmer tube and asked for the lower section to be slightly shorter. Its endpoint is 1.5 mm above the previous visual registration. The new chamber keeps its generated aspect ratio; it is not squeezed into the old fine-mist chamber envelope. The clear seated tube uses a 62% multiply layer so its chamber remains visible through the glass. The separate exploded layer retains natural unblended plastic material. Other glass colors retain their existing assembled visibility rules.

A silhouette-only SVG clip excludes the generation's exterior haze; generated material pixels remain inside the contour. The chamber's visual width, tube diameter and source-photo proportions are registration targets, not measured manufacturer specifications. Native Blender geometry and source photographs remain preserved. The final raw source and transparent registered master are archived as candidates for review.

Flair's former scale landmark lies below the neck/shoulder crest. Sprayers, matching overcaps and smooth caps now meet source image row 518, canvas Y 549.0236747297993. Ribbed Flair caps retain their prior vertical seating. The internal sprayer tube still ends at the same floor; its upper attachment follows the lifted collar. Flair body geometry and metal/plastic roller fit are unchanged. The two source-held Minaret variants remain outside the available layered kits.

The tall 9 ml short ribbed caps now end at canvas Y 337, covering the last thread. Black/white and clear/frosted share this adjustment. Approved 5 ml ribbed caps are unchanged.
