# Tall Cylinder 9 ml, 13-415: pour-cap alignment

Scope is the 20 clear/frosted pour-cap combinations shown by the existing 9 ml 13-415 product pages. This excludes the wider 9 ml 17-415 body, sprayers, roll-ons and every other bottle family.

The visible cap centers were consistently about 2.7 to 2.8 units left of the registered bottle axis on the 1000 x 1100 canvas. Transparent source margins also left some visible bottom edges about 0.3 canvas units above their recorded seating line. Identical image-box placement did not align the actual product pixels.

The correction measures each immutable cap source at alpha >= 128, centers its visible silhouette on the existing bottle axis and brings the visible bottom to its reviewed contact line. Lined caps keep the existing shoulder reference; ribbed caps keep their final-thread reference. Source boxes move by at most 2.811085414 units horizontally and 0.30243729 units vertically. Cap widths, heights, aspect ratios, pixels, finishes and existing seating targets are preserved. No unmeasured width or height rescale is applied.

Both bottle bodies, anchors, approved 62.5% shoulder sizing, 91% baseline and all component source hashes are unchanged. Catalog, product page and builder share the corrected hosted recipe. Existing localhost review files and bookmarked pilots use those same current recipes, while still returning no local candidate when its source files are absent.

The manifest retains 456 configurations, with only these 20 cap placements revised. All other 436 recipes are checked against their prior hashes. Historical approval tests reconstruct the earlier records for this documented later alignment, and the new regression separately verifies the current visible cap edges. The versioned manifest/index is 4c6095d23d920c4f.
