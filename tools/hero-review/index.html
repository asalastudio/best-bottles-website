How to give an exact height

The grid measures a percentage of the full image height upward from the shared 91% baseline. For example, an 80% tall bottle reaches 11% from the top. Click the image to place a target line, or enter a precise number below it. Choose “Bottle including top,” “Glass body only,” or “Glass base to shoulder,” then Save sizing request. This records a guide; it does not resize or stretch the image.

For shoulder-aligned revisions, the blue guide measures from the glass base to its shoulder. Verified matching bottle bodies share a guide across fitments, derived from your earlier overall-size preferences. Shoulder requests apply to the matching group in the next correction pass; tall and short 9 mL bottles remain separate.

Bulbs, tassels and separate caps must still fit inside the frame when the entire assembly is uniformly scaled. Above 88% leaves less than 3% top clearance and needs extra clipping checks. Blue lines show saved or proposed targets. Measured values are shown when a verified sizing pass exists; clicking the image records a request, not an automatic measurement.

Google’s guidance is 75–90% image occupancy; this height grid is our own precise review convention.
