# 17-415 neck-thread matrix — source reconciliation

The [17 × 11 in print PDF](17-415-neck-thread-matrix-17x11.pdf) and [300 dpi PNG](17-415-neck-thread-matrix-17x11-print.png) group components by **17-415 neck finish**, then connect them to the five current 9 mL Cylinder glass finishes. The component pictures come from the exact 17-415 images in the master closure references; the bottle pictures are current cap-off source plates, scaled against the website's published height without cap. The cap-off plates still show the roller insert seated in the neck, which is identified on the sheet.

The [Best Bottles accessories selector](https://www.bestbottles.com/all-bottles/accessories/caps-plugs-sprayers.php) presents **10 roll-on outer cap variants** (7 solid finishes and 3 dotted), **6 fine-mist sprayer finishes**, and **3 lotion/treatment pump finishes**. The black, pink, and silver dotted designs are variants within the outer-cap group. Plastic-ball and metal-ball roller inserts are different underlying assembly materials, not two more cap colors. Source photos for each material were taken from current clear 9 mL Cylinder cap-off plates; this export does not establish standalone insert products.

The five [representative product pages and source images](body-groups.json) each list **17-415**: clear, amber, cobalt blue, frosted, and swirl. Clear, amber, and cobalt list 70 mm without cap; frosted and swirl list 74 mm. The current read-only [Convex 17-415 export](convex-17-415.json) contains 145 active Cylinder glass assemblies: 29 for each finish, comprising 10 plastic roller + 10 metal roller + 6 spray + 3 pump combinations. The [record-level bottle CSV](convex-bottle-records.csv) preserves exact SKU identities. The sheet shows one representative photo per glass finish so component imagery is not repeated across 145 assembled SKUs.

The [component inventory](component-inventory.csv) covers all 21 component records returned with a 17-415 label. The [remedy register](component-remedy-register.csv) distinguishes verified source discrepancies from unresolved identity questions. Three `Ltn17-415*` treatment pumps are currently classified as `Roll-On Cap`; the ten `CpRoll17-415*` caps are labeled only for a plastic roller even though metal-roller assemblies exist. A blank-SKU black dotted cap record may duplicate `CpRoll17-415BlkDot` and needs ID reconciliation before any deletion. `Droppers1ozElg` is labeled 17-415 in Convex while [its website product page](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/dropper-black-rubber-bulb-black-trim-glass-pipette-15ml) states **18-400**, so it is excluded from the compatibility rail.

The single `GBPillar9SpryBlkMatt` glass record is labeled 17-415 in Convex, but its linked public page does not expose a verifiable neck specification; other Pillar 9 mL records are 13-415. It is held out pending SKU/source confirmation. The master PSD archive also contains a black 9 mL Cylinder finish that is absent from this current 17-415 Convex glass export; it is not counted as an active sixth finish without a current sellability check. Two retired Swirl roll-on rows remain in the export but are not counted among the 145 active Cylinder assemblies.

Matching the thread identifies a candidate compatibility set. Exact assembly identity, roller insert seating, and spray or pump dip-tube length still require SKU-level verification. These findings are catalog identity and association risks, not evidence that a component physically fails to fit.

No Convex, Shopify, or live website data was changed. The diagram is a deterministic PDF composition of source images, measurements, and counts; no generative model redrew the bottle geometry or labels.
