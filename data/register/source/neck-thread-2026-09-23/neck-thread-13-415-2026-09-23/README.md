## Cap identity resolution

The legacy accessories selector has **10 standard cap choices**: 2 short ribbed (black, white), 6 **short liner caps** (the black already shown plus 5 additional copper/gold/silver finishes), and 2 **tall liner caps** (gold, silver). The owner's five short-line caps are therefore the five additional finishes beyond the black short liner cap. “Liner cap” names the closure category on this sheet; shiny or matte finish does not establish that a cap is made entirely of metal. The owner's “phonodic plastic” description appears to refer to **phenolic plastic**, a material named on [Best Bottles' 18-415 cap pages](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Caps-reducer-lids-top-bottle-shiny-gold-Color-18-415); material for each 13-415 SKU still needs its own source check. See [cap-identity-review.csv](cap-identity-review.csv) for every website option ID.

The tall silver `CP13-415Sl` and tall gold `CP13-415Gl` are **not duplicates** of the short shiny silver/gold caps and are not roll-on caps. Their [silver](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Caps-lids-top-bottle-shiny-silver-Color-13-415) and [gold](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Caps-lids-top-bottle-shiny-gold-Color-13-415) component pages give a 24 mm cap height and describe a foam liner. The same 5 mL Cylinder body is [65 mm with the regular gold cap](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/cylinder-design-5-ml-glass-bottle-shiny-gold-cap) and [59 mm with the short shiny gold cap](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/cylinder-design-5-ml-glass-bottle-short-shiny-gold-cap). That 6 mm assembled-height difference supports a genuinely different cap profile, even though color and thread are shared.

The [accessories source page](https://www.bestbottles.com/all-bottles/accessories/caps-plugs-sprayers.php) shows 9 roll-on caps: 6 solid colors/finishes and 3 dotted options (pink, black, silver). The dotted options remain variants within **one roll-on cap group**. Plastic and metal roller inserts are separate parts underneath that outer cap. The same source page shows 8 fine-mist sprayer finishes. Its exact option IDs/images were captured in the previous [website-count-evidence.json](../cylinder-matrix-sheet-2026-09-23/component-counts/website-count-evidence.json).

## Current data and action

Read-only Convex export on 2026-09-23 returned 2,540 products, including 583 records labeled 13-415. Of those, 538 are Glass Bottle records (31 marked retired). The [full 13-415 snapshot](convex-13-415.json) and [glass-bottle CSV](convex-bottle-records.csv) preserve exact SKU evidence. This print plate uses one shape/size representative rather than drawing assembled finish/color variants repeatedly.

The [component remedy register](component-remedy-register.csv) records the next data fixes. In particular, Convex currently places `CP13-415Gl`, `CP13-415Sl`, `CP13-415BlkSht`, and `CP13-415WhtSht` in the **Roll-On Cap family**, even though their item names/applicator identify ordinary closure caps. The six short liner-cap options are not cleanly represented as six exact component records in this export. The nine roll-on cap records are marked with a plastic-roller applicator despite the owner's confirmation that the outer caps also cover metal roller assemblies. These are **catalog identity and association risks**, not proof of a physical misfit.

The master PSD archive also has 13-415 folders for Queen 10 mL, Daisy 10 mL, Footed Rectangle 15 mL, one-dram, `GB2o3`, and `GB1o5 Amber` that do not appear among the current Convex 13-415 glass rows. Some are visible on the site's UAT pages; active sellability and exact identity still need checking before connecting them on a production compatibility sheet. The archive labels Bell as 12 mL while the [current live Bell page](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/bell-design-10-ml-glass-bottle-short-shiny-black-cap) lists 10 mL. This plate uses the live 10 mL identity and flags the archive label as historical drift.

No Convex, Shopify, or live website data was changed. The diagram uses source images and deterministic PDF layout; no generative image model was used for this correction, so counts, labels and geometry remain auditable.
