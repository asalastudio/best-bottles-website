# Small-format neck matrix — 23 September 2026

The [one-page 17 × 11 in landscape PDF](small-format-neck-matrix-13-425-8-425-12mm-17x11.pdf) combines the requested small-vial and Cylinder neck formats. The [300 dpi preview](small-format-neck-matrix-print.png) is suitable for visual review. The three lanes are **13-425**, **8-425**, and **12 mm**; 14-425 is noted separately because the current Convex development export has no matching bottle or component row. These are separate neck standards, not one interchangeable component pool.

The [read-only Convex export](convex-small-format.json) has 2,540 total products and these exact matching rows:

| 8-425 | 4 | 5 | Clear/amber 2 mL vials and five distinct short/tall closure choices |
| 12 mm | 4 | 0 | 3 and 4 mL Cylinder spray assemblies, each with black and white tops |
| 14-425 | 0 | 0 | No current record; no component association inferred |

The images come from exact SKU source plates in [source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json) and the existing local 8-425 closure references. Source silhouettes were cropped and placed, not regenerated. Within the 13-425 bottle row, the 2/3/4 mL heights follow the published vial dimensions approximately; component crops are enlarged for identification. The 12 mm sprayer images are **assembled bottle evidence**, because Convex contains no standalone 12 mm sprayer SKU. The 13-425 black/white cap crops are from exact vial assemblies, since the two cap-coded Convex rows have no reliable source image or product URL.

Agent Reach’s Jina web reader checked [all 31 current product links plus the blue white-cap source variant](agent-reach-site-verification.csv). Of 32 reads, **27 matched the exact SKU, thread, and listed capacity**; two 12 mm 3 mL sprays matched SKU/thread but had the known Convex `3.3` versus website/master `3` capacity drift; two 13-425 cap links resolved to unrelated products; and the blue black-cap vial’s stored URL returned a product-unavailable page. The [page captures](site-pages) and their SHA-256 values are retained in the verification CSV. A search result cached the blue black-cap page as 2 mL, but the current page is unavailable, so that exact page is not counted as a live confirmation. The [repo master catalog](../../Nemat_Product_Catalog.csv) agrees with the current Convex thread labels for all 31 rows, but it also carries the two misdirected cap links. This is why matching local labels alone was insufficient.

The source-backed review points are in [review-issues.csv](review-issues.csv), with every matching Convex row in [current-records.csv](current-records.csv):

The current [white-cap blue assembly](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Vial-design-half-dram-blue-glass-white-short-cap) confirms 2 mL and 13-425 but is absent from the 16 current 13-425 bottle rows. Both require exact-SKU reconciliation before a product-data correction.

This is a source and catalog-identity reference. Thread equality identifies candidates; it does not prove stem length, liner, seating, or every exact sellable pairing. No product or component records were modified for this new sheet.
