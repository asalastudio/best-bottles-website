## Source and count basis

The [read-only Convex export](convex-18-415.json) contains 1,423 records labeled 18-415 after the exact frosted Circle reducer correction. It includes 64 component records and 1,359 bottle-category records; 94 bottle records are marked retired. The 1,265 current bottle-category records comprise **1,263 shared-matrix assemblies** across 22 body/size formats and **two fixed 30 mL spray products**. The corrected `GBCrclFrst50RdcrIvyLthr` increases the frosted Circle 50 mL format count from 35 to 36. One representative per body/finish was checked against a public Best Bottles page in [body-groups.json](body-groups.json): 29 of 29 pages show 18-415. Each body card uses an existing source plate or master PSD composite. Its measurement is the website's published height without cap. The picture retains its attached source sprayer/pump where present and is sized for recognition, not printed at 1:1 physical scale.

The [Best Bottles accessories selector](https://www.bestbottles.com/all-bottles/accessories/caps-plugs-sprayers.php) contains 11 cap choices: five faux-leather caps and six choices grouped here under the **working label “travel / liner caps.”** The website calls individual choices caps or closures and describes foam liners; it also uses “travel cap” for protective caps shipped with [vintage sprayers](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Vintage-antique-bulb-sprayer-Black-Tassel). This catalog label is a useful grouping pending final product terminology, not a claim that every option is sold for travel use. The previous “metal caps” label overgeneralized a material detail: the [short matte-silver cap](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Caps-reducer-lids-top-bottle-matte-silver-18-415) has a metal shell and plastic inner, while the [shiny-gold cap](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Caps-reducer-lids-top-bottle-shiny-gold-Color-18-415) is described as hard plastic/phenolic with a foam liner.

The selector also contains six fine-mist sprayers, eight untasseled vintage bulb sprayers, nine tasseled bulbs, and six pumps; a matte-gold pump has a separate listing. Three 18-415 droppers are supported by individual product pages, for example the [silver dropper](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/Dropper-white-rubber-bulb-silver-trim-glass-Pipette). The master closure references and assembled bottle pages add a **short shiny-black cap seen on an assembled bottle**, beyond the six travel / liner-cap selector choices; its standalone selector status is unverified. They also show a white untasseled vintage bulb beyond the selector group: [shiny-black cap assembly](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/cylinder-design-100-ml-glass-bottle-reducer-black-shiny-cap), [white bulb assembly](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/diva-design-30-ml-glass-bottle-white-vintage-style-bulb-sprayer-shiny-silver-collar-cap). That yields **46 source-evidenced outer variants** on the PDF: 12 caps (five faux-leather, six selectable travel / liner caps, one assembly-evidenced short shiny-black cap), six mist sprayers, seven pumps, nine untasseled bulbs, nine tasseled bulbs, and three droppers. The reducer is a **separate insert**, not a 47th outer finish; [reducer-plus-cap products](https://www.bestbottles.com/product/cylinder-design-100-ml-glass-bottle-reducer-silver-matte-cap) establish the assembly structure.
