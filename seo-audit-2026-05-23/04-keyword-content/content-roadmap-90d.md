# Best Bottles — 90-Day Content Roadmap (Weeks 13-26)

**Audit lead:** Asala Studio · Jordan Richter (jordan@asala.ai)
**Date:** 2026-05-23
**Target launch:** 2026-06-15
**Scope:** A 14-week, 28-piece content roadmap that picks up where `docs/SEO_CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` (Weeks 1-12, 24 posts) leaves off. This document covers Weeks 13-26 — the first quarter post-launch — and is structured around the 5 personas and 150-keyword corpus in `persona-keyword-corpus.md`.

This roadmap does NOT duplicate any of the 24 posts in the existing calendar. Where the new pieces interlink with existing posts, those existing posts are cited by week number and title for the editorial team's reference.

---

## Strategic frame for Weeks 13-26

The existing 12-week calendar built the foundation: brand authority, product spotlights, technical authority, and the "why glass" case. The next quarter has three jobs:

1. **Pillar the existing post equity.** Six existing posts are doing the work of explainer content but live in the blog (low SERP authority). Wrap them in pillar pages on `/resources/` that the existing posts link UP to. Each pillar earns category-level rankings the individual blog posts cannot.

2. **Open the procurement persona for the first time.** Persona 2 is invisible in the Weeks 1-12 calendar. Weeks 13-26 build the `/services/contract-packaging` page (the Stage 3 P0 critical migration), customer-story content modeled on Container & Packaging Supply, and procurement-readiness pillars.

3. **Push to AEO citation share.** Every new piece uses question-shaped titles, named-author bylines, dated content stamps, Q+A blocks at the top, and FAQPage schema — the Stage 2 AEO patterns. The 28 pieces below double Best Bottles' indexable surface from 33 indexable pages (9 blog + 24 calendar + ~10 utility) to 60+ within the quarter.
