## How to use this spec

Each clip is generated in Higgsfield's video model with the full prompt below. The prompts are written for Higgsfield's strengths: cinematic product photography, intimate macro, ASMR pacing, ambient documentary feel. They deliberately avoid talking-head AI generation (which is uncanny and credibility-damaging for this audience) — every "founder voice" idea pairs a Higgsfield establishing/B-roll clip with real founder audio recorded separately.

**Generation settings (suggested defaults):**
- Model: Higgsfield Lite or Higgsfield Pro depending on clip complexity
- Output format: MP4
- Quality: 1080p minimum
- Sound: generate without audio; sound design added in post (cap clicks, glass tinks, dropper squeezes are sourced from a separate library or recorded in-house)

**Reusability scoring (1–10):** how many distinct social posts this single clip can produce. 10 = pin + Reel + TikTok + LinkedIn brand post + carousel slide + still-frame variants. 1 = single-post use only.

## Production notes for the batch

**Sound design layer (post-production, NOT generated by Higgsfield):**
- ASMR clips (HF-001 to HF-005): record actual cap clicks, glass tinks, dropper squeezes, atomizer mist sounds in-house with a Zoom H6 or Rode NTG3. ~2 hour recording session captures everything needed for all 5 clips plus reusable library.
- Entertainment clips (HF-011 to HF-015): Abbas or a brand spokesperson records voiceover for each clip. Script per clip is ~30 words, total ~150 words of VO. ~1 hour recording session.
- Interview clips (HF-021 to HF-025): the real audio is captured at the actual trade shows. The Higgsfield clip provides establishing-shot mood until real footage is captured. Until then, the clips are usable as standalone brand atmosphere posts.
- Review clips (HF-016 to HF-020): customer-founder voiceover recorded separately during onboarding to the customer-story program.

**Music layer (optional):** if a music bed is added, use Soundstripe or Artlist licensed tracks. Avoid trending TikTok audio for B2B content — it's the fastest way to look unserious.

- End-card: 1-second hold with `bestbottles.com` URL in clean serif type, "Beautifully Contained." tagline below
- Lower-third on educational clips: small `Best Bottles` brand mark, never overpowering the content
- No watermarks during the clip body — the brand earns the view first, then claims credit at the end

- Master 9:16 export becomes IG Reels + TikTok native
